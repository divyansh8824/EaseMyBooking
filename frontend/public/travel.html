FAQ

Before you book

Real questions our travellers ask us before saying yes.

Ask on WhatsApp
Are the package prices final?

The price shown is a "starts from" indicative cost per person on twin sharing for the standard category. Your final quote depends on travel dates, hotel category, group size and customisations — we send a transparent itemised quote on WhatsApp within 30 minutes of your enquiry.

Can you customise these packages?

Absolutely. 90% of our bookings are customised — extra nights, hotel upgrades, private transfers, specific experiences, dietary preferences. Just share what you want and we'll redesign the itinerary.

What is included in the package?

A standard package includes accommodation, daily breakfast, airport transfers, sightseeing on private/SIC basis, and 24×7 concierge support. Flights, visas and travel insurance are optional add-ons (we book those too).

How do I pay?

We accept UPI, NEFT, all major credit/debit cards and EMI on most cards. A 25% booking advance secures your trip — balance 7 days before departure.

What about visa & insurance?

We provide complete visa assistance (document checklist, application, appointment, follow-up) for 60+ countries and bundle Bajaj/HDFC ERGO travel insurance with every international booking.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 30 days before departure on most packages. 50% refund 15-30 days prior, no refund within 15 days. Flight, visa and insurance fees are non-refundable per provider rules. Detailed policy shared with your quote.
