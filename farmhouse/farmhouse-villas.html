Frequently Asked

Everything you'd want to know before booking your private estate.

How do I book a villa or farmhouse?

Browse our curated collection, tap "Enquire Now" on any property, and submit your dates. Our concierge confirms availability over WhatsApp within 30 minutes, sends a custom quote, and secures your booking.

Are meals and staff included?

Most estates include a full-time caretaker and chef. Meal packages are billed separately based on the menu you choose. Premium services like in-villa bartenders, butlers, and decor are available on request.

Can I host a wedding or large event?

Absolutely — many of our farmhouses host 200-600 guests with full event production. Mention "Wedding / Event" in your enquiry and we'll share a tailored proposal including decor, sound, catering and vendor management.

Is there a security deposit?

Yes, a refundable security deposit (typically ₹25,000 – ₹1,00,000 depending on property) is collected at check-in and refunded within 7 days post-checkout subject to property condition.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 14 days before check-in for most properties. 50% refund 7-14 days prior. No refund within 7 days. Event bookings follow a custom policy shared during quotation.

Can pets stay at the property?

Several of our farmhouses are pet-friendly. Look for the "Pet Friendly" amenity badge or mention your furry guest in the enquiry — we'll confirm before booking.
