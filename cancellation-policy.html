Cancellation Policy

EaseMyBooking

At EaseMyBooking, we strive to provide a seamless experience for event bookings, venue reservations, curated travel experiences, and vendor coordination. Please read our cancellation policy carefully before making a booking.

1. Booking Confirmation

A booking is considered confirmed only after advance payment or token amount is received and officially acknowledged by EaseMyBooking.

2. Cancellation by Customer

  • More than 30 days before event: Partial refund may be applicable depending on vendor terms.
  • 15–30 days before event: Cancellation charges may apply up to 50% of booking amount.
  • Less than 15 days before event: Advance amount may become non-refundable due to vendor commitments.

3. Venue & Vendor Cancellation Terms

Venue owners, decorators, caterers, photographers, hotels, travel providers, and other vendors may have their own cancellation policies. Refund eligibility will depend on the vendor’s individual terms and commitments already made.

4. Non-Refundable Services

  • Customized decorations
  • Pre-booked hotels or travel reservations
  • Artist/performer advance payments
  • Special arrangements or personalized services
  • Last-minute bookings

5. Cancellation by EaseMyBooking

In rare cases, EaseMyBooking reserves the right to cancel a booking due to unavoidable circumstances, vendor unavailability, force majeure events, or operational limitations. In such cases, reasonable alternatives or refunds will be discussed with the customer.

6. Refund Timeline

Approved refunds (if applicable) are generally processed within 7–15 business days depending on banking channels and vendor settlements.

7. Force Majeure

EaseMyBooking shall not be liable for cancellations or delays caused due to natural disasters, government restrictions, pandemics, weather conditions, strikes, political disruptions, or circumstances beyond reasonable control.
