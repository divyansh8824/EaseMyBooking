Vendor Agreement

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

This Vendor Agreement ("Agreement") governs the relationship between EaseMyBooking ("Platform", "Company", "We", "Us") and the Vendor ("Service Provider", "Partner", "Vendor"). By registering or listing services on EaseMyBooking, vendors agree to comply with all terms mentioned below.

2. Vendor Eligibility

To become a registered vendor on EaseMyBooking:

  • Vendor must provide genuine business details.
  • Vendor must provide valid ID proof and business proof if requested.
  • Vendor should possess legal authorization to provide services.
  • Vendor agrees not to submit misleading pricing or fake information.

3. Service Quality Commitment

Vendor agrees to:

  • Deliver promised services professionally.
  • Maintain quality standards and customer satisfaction.
  • Provide accurate pricing and avoid hidden charges.
  • Be punctual and maintain communication with clients.

4. Pricing & Payments

  • EaseMyBooking may charge platform fees or commissions.
  • Final pricing must be agreed before booking confirmation.
  • Vendor shall not charge unauthorized hidden costs.
  • Payment timelines will be communicated separately.

5. Booking Confirmation

Vendors are responsible for honoring confirmed bookings. Once a booking is accepted, cancellation without valid reason may lead to penalties or account suspension.

6. Cancellation Policy

  • Vendor must inform cancellation immediately.
  • Repeated cancellations may result in temporary or permanent removal.
  • Emergency situations will be reviewed case-by-case.

7. Prohibited Activities

  • Fraudulent activities.
  • Fake reviews or manipulated ratings.
  • Misrepresentation of services.
  • Customer harassment or misconduct.
  • Sharing false availability or fake quotations.

8. Liability

EaseMyBooking acts as a facilitator between vendors and customers. The platform is not directly responsible for vendor performance, delays, damages, or disputes arising from vendor services.

9. Account Suspension

EaseMyBooking reserves the right to suspend or terminate vendor accounts for misconduct, poor service quality, repeated complaints, fake commitments, or policy violations.

EaseMyBooking reserves the right to modify this agreement at any time. Vendors are responsible for reviewing updates periodically.
